MomShell

A cross-university project exploring how AI can support communication and childcare collaboration in postpartum families.

Jiayi Wang · Team lead · AI Hackathon Tour 2026

From a philosophical question to a practical project

Inspired by feminist perspectives in philosophy of science, our team began with a concern about the demands and communication challenges that postpartum families face. MomShell explores how AI can help translate a mother’s expressed needs into practical suggestions for her partner, supporting shared childcare. Its design includes a low-effort way for mothers to share how they feel and a task-oriented view that helps partners understand how they can contribute.

A nationwide AI challenge

AI Hackathon Tour 2026 was jointly launched by ModelScope and Datawhale. According to the organizers, the university league ran for more than 100 days, involved 11 universities and attracted over 5,600 participants. The national final was held in Nanjing.

Event figures: ModelScope, final recap (Chinese).

I led a cross-university team of students from Fudan University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University. MomShell placed third in the Shanghai Jiao Tong University regional competition and advanced to the national final.

Why this project matters to me

MomShell was an opportunity to translate a question about gender, care and information into a concrete design challenge. It brought together philosophical reflection, cross-disciplinary collaboration and hands-on AI application development.
